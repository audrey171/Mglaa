Source

Yamaha DX7-inspired sounds

The sampled voices come from FreePats. They were recorded from the Hexter and Dexed software synthesizers, which emulate the Yamaha DX7. They are not recordings supplied by Yamaha or sampled directly from DX7 hardware. The sustained pads are synthesized in the browser.

  • FM Synthesized Piano #1 — Roberto / FreePats; recorded with Hexter. Used for the soft and classic electric piano voices.
  • FM Synthesized Piano #2 — Roberto / FreePats; recorded with Hexter. Used for the bright electric piano voices.
  • Synth Brass #2 — Strix SoundFont Team / FreePats; recorded with Dexed, emulating the DX7 “BRASS 7” patch.
  • Synth Crystal — Strix SoundFont Team / FreePats; recorded with Dexed.

The sample sets use the CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication. For this instrument, samples were trimmed to at most four seconds, converted to mono 32 kHz PCM, gain-balanced, and faded at the end. Sample credits, licenses, and provenance.

Weaving patterns

Miniature Patterns for Hand Weaving, Josephine E. Estes. Scans hosted by the University of Arizona’s weaving archive:

  • Part One (PDF)
  • Part Two (PDF)

The instrument includes 48 named patterns across both books. Each draft’s source page and selected variation are recorded in the pattern library.
