Instructions

Choose a pattern and two colors, then press play.

Pattern
Click the pattern name to choose a draft. Choose Custom to draw in the top grid: click or drag to move the filled square in each column.
Colors
Open either swatch. The grid changes hue and lightness; the strip below changes saturation. The two small swatches switch which color you’re editing. Click outside to close.
Saved colors
Tap an empty slot in the bottom row to save a color. Tap a saved swatch to apply it; tap the selected slot again to replace it. With two or more saved colors, applying one starts a loop through the row. Picking a color manually or changing patterns stops the loop. Saved colors stay between visits.
Sound
The upper-right grid selects instruments: melody on the left, pad on the right. Both follow the harmony made by your colors.
Playback
Click play/pause or press Space. While paused, scroll or drag to explore earlier rows. Press play to return to the newest work. Switching tabs keeps playback running, though phone browsers may interrupt background audio.
Export
Export opens a PDF preview in a new tab; download saves it. The file includes all rows you’ve played, including those off-screen, and excludes the initial preview.
