Instructions

Choose a pattern and two colors. Together they make a repeating textile and a tune. A new session opens on Pond Lily.

Pattern
Click the blue pattern name at the top left to choose one of 48 drafts from Miniature Patterns for Hand Weaving, or choose Custom. Each named pattern starts with its own two colors and a melody/pad instrument pairing.
Threading grid
The four rows at the top show which shaft each warp thread belongs to. Read the rows from bottom to top as shafts 1–4. In Custom, click or drag to move the filled square in each column. Left and right arrows choose a column; up and down arrows change its shaft. Custom repeats over 32 columns. Short drafts repeat across the display; long drafts are cut off at a whole square. Their full sequences still generate the pattern.
Two colors
Click either small color square below the threading grid. The first colors the pattern; the second colors the ground. Both swatches appear inside the panel; click either to switch which color you are editing without closing it. In the palette, move horizontally for hue and vertically for lightness. The strip below changes saturation. Click the single color square above the palette for an exact color picker. Arrow keys also move through the palette and saturation strip. Both colors contribute to the music.
Instruments
The upper-right grid has 32 squares. Its left four columns choose among 16 melody sounds; its right four columns choose among 16 sustained pad sounds. One square stays selected in each half. Click to select, or move with the arrow keys while a square is focused. Your palette and instrument choices stay with each pattern during this tab’s session.
Play and pause
Click the blue underlined play/pause control at the bottom left, or press Space. About stays at the bottom right. Escape closes an open palette; otherwise it pauses the instrument. Changing a color, draft, or instrument lets you shape the sound while it plays. Color and pattern changes restart the phrase immediately, with a soft transition. Sound pauses when you leave the instrument.
Export
Click export beside about to preview the entire pattern as a PDF. Choose download to save it, or back to return to the instrument. Playback pauses at the moment of export. The file includes the starting textile and every row added, including those that have scrolled off-screen. Newest rows appear first, just as on screen; long patterns continue across pages. Squares stay square, with the original colors and grid lines. The only text is a small blue mglaa.com link. The PDF is made in your browser.
The scrolling pattern
New rows enter at the top and move down as the music plays. Pattern changes, custom-draft edits, and color changes apply to new rows; earlier rows keep their original pattern and colors. Your scroll stays intact when you visit About and return. The small draft and its repeating harness sequence generate the larger pattern. Each square is a thread crossing, rather than an individual musical note.
